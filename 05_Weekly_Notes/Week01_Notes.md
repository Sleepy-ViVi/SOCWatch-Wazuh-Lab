## 4. Risks Identified

### Risks Identified

The following potential risks were identified during the planning phase:

- Unexpected AWS usage and costs.
- Incorrect AWS security group rules.
- Accidental exposure of administrative ports.
- Exposure of passwords, private keys, or other credentials.
- Connectivity problems between the Wazuh manager and endpoint agents.
